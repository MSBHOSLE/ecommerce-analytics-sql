An advanced **PostgreSQL Business Intelligence & Analytics Suite** featuring **Recursive Common Table Expressions (CTEs)**, cumulative running total window functions, **RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) Customer Segmentation**, and cohort retention matrices visualized in a Streamlit BI dashboard.

---

## ⚡ Highlights

- **RFM Customer Segmentation**: Quantile-based classification of accounts into Champions, Loyal, At-Risk, and Churned segments.
- **Window Functions**: Cumulative running totals, lead/lag period-over-period growth comparisons, and dense rankings.
- **Cohort Retention Modeling**: Month-by-month user cohort activity tracking.
